About Corasel

Celebrate. Explore.
Belong.

A city is more than its streets and landmarks. It's the people you share it with. Corasel exists to help you get out, try something new, and make the most of where you live, whether you've been here for ten years or ten days.

We host and curate events across the city: night walks past glowing monuments, gallery hops, trivia nights, creative workshops, cozy dinners, game nights, and seasonal parties. Some are quiet and some are loud. Some are free and some are ticketed. All of them are designed so you can show up on your own and feel at home.

What we do

🎉

We celebrate.

Birthdays, new jobs, new neighborhoods, long weekends, Halloween, a good Thursday. We create reasons to gather and make the small moments feel like occasions.

🤝

We bring people together.

Conversation comes easily when there's something to do, like a game, a walk, a canvas, or a shared table. You don't need an opener, only curiosity.

🧭

We help you explore.

From hidden cafés and neighborhood galleries to monument views after dark, we show you the side of your city that guidebooks miss.

🌱

We create community.

The goal isn't a one-off night out. It's a circle of familiar faces, regular rituals, and friendships that carry on after the event ends.

What we believe

✨

Everyone belongs.

There’s no right way to show up. You might be new to the city, a longtime local, out on your own, or surrounded by friends. You might be quiet, curious, outgoing, or somewhere in between. Wherever you are in life, there’s a place for you here.

☕

Real places beat screens.

The best memories are created in real neighborhoods, with real people, over good food, shared stories, and genuine conversations. There’s always something beyond putting your phone down, stepping outside, and experiencing a place together. The moments worth capturing on a screen are usually the ones that happen off the screen.

⭐

Good hosting makes the difference.

Walking into a new space is easier when there is a proper welcome and genuine care. We strive to create the warmest, most welcoming setting and make sure everyone has a chance to feel included. Because sometimes, all it takes is one friendly face to make a new place feel a little more like home.

🛡️

Safe and inclusive by design.

Every connection starts with feeling comfortable and cared for. That means choosing welcoming spaces, setting clear expectations, and creating a culture where people look out for one another. Everyone should feel safe enough to be themselves and enjoy being part of the moment.

💬

Built for you, with you.

The best communities grow by learning from the people who are part of them. After every event, we want to know what you enjoyed, what could be better, and what you’d love to experience next. Your feedback helps us shape what comes next, because you’re not just part of the community—you’re part of what makes it better.
